About

I'm a marketer and photographer based in Phoenix. I lead marketing at Culdesac, the first car-free neighborhood built from scratch in the US.

My job is turning what we're building into stories people want to follow: organic and paid social, press, events, and decks for leadership speaking engagements. I've coordinated visits and coverage from outlets like The New York Times, CNN, and The Daily Show, and I shoot a lot of the photo and video myself.

Before Culdesac, I worked in the City of Phoenix Mayor's Office. My background is in sustainability and GIS.
